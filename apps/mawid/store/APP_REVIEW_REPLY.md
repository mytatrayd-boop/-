Hello, thank you for reviewing our app.

1. Screen recording: attached. It was recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts from launching the app and shows the main flow. The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content, and no paid content or in-app purchases.

2. Purpose and target audience: "مواعيد الرواتب والإجازات" (Salary & Holiday Dates) is an Arabic-language utility for people in Saudi Arabia. It shows how many days remain until the next government salary (27th), the housing support payment (24th), and other monthly payment dates. It adjusts each date automatically when it falls on a weekend. It also shows official public holidays and the school calendar on a monthly calendar. The problem it solves: people currently calculate these dates by hand, or rely on rumors on social media. The app gives them a clear countdown, a budget split of the amount, and reminders 3 days and 1 day before each date.

3. Setup and instructions: no login or setup is needed. Open the app. The Home tab shows the countdown to the next payment. The Month tab shows the calendar with holidays. Tap a payment to see its details and the budget split. Under "Customize" (تخصيص) you can choose which payments to show, change the app color, and enable reminders. You can also share the countdown card as an image, or add dates to the iOS Calendar.

4. External services: the app downloads a public, read-only JSON file of holiday dates from raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub), so the school calendar can be updated without a new app version. It keeps a local copy, so it works offline. There is no analytics, no advertising, no authentication, no payment processor, and no AI service. Reminders are local notifications that are scheduled on the device. All data stays on the device.

5. Regional differences: the content is designed for Saudi Arabia (Saudi payment dates and holidays). The app works the same way in every region. Nothing changes based on the user's location.

6. Regulated industry: not applicable. The app is not a banking or financial service. It does not move money or connect to any bank account. It only shows publicly announced dates. It contains no protected third-party material.

Thank you.
