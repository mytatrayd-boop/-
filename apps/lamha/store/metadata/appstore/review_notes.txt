Lamha is an Arabic home-screen and lock-screen widget app: Hijri and Gregorian dates, prayer times, Qibla compass, and payment dates for salaries and benefits. No account or login is required.

Subscriptions: monthly (lamha.monthly) and yearly (lamha.yearly), both with a 7-day free trial, in one subscription group. The subscription screen appears on first launch. Tap "Start free trial" and complete the sandbox purchase; the app then unlocks. "Restore purchases" and the privacy policy link are on the same screen. The app verifies the signed transaction on its server.

The subscription screen also has an optional "I have an activation code" entry. These codes are given personally by the developer to a few acquaintances and testers; reviewers do not need one.

To see the widget: long-press the Home Screen, tap "+" (or Edit > Add Widget), then choose Lamha.

Payout dates are computed on-device from published rules; the app is independent and not affiliated with any government entity. Weather is fetched from Open-Meteo using the chosen city's coordinates.
